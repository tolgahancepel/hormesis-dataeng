Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Claude Certified Architect (Foundations) exam?

The Claude Certified Architect (Foundations) exam is Anthropic's official certification for developers who build with Claude. It covers 5 domains: Agentic Architecture, Tool Design & MCP, Claude Code Configuration, Prompt Engineering, and Context Management. You need 720 out of 1,000 points to pass.

Is this study guide free?

Yes, the Claude Certification Guide is completely free. It includes 30 lessons, 150+ practice questions, a full mock exam, hands-on build exercises, and quick reference cards — all available without sign-up.

How do I prepare for the Claude certification exam?

Start with the diagnostic assessment to identify your weak areas, then work through the lessons for each of the 5 domains. Use the mock exam to simulate real exam conditions, and practise with the build exercises for hands-on experience.

How long does it take to prepare for the Claude exam?

Most developers with Claude experience need 15-20 hours of study. If you are new to Claude, allow 30-40 hours. The diagnostic test helps you focus on the domains where you need the most work.

Is this guide affiliated with Anthropic?

No. This is an independent community resource. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Anthropic. The content is based on official Anthropic documentation, Skilljar courses, and the published exam guide.
